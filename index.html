The Pie Chart Allocation

Reflect on your team's contribution. Distribute 100% of the project credit across your group members based on their contributions to the group project. Make sure to give a justification for this rationale in the comment box as well.

How should I think about allocation?

Before assigning each person's percentage, consider how they performed across the following areas:

Group Work

Did this person carry their share of the workload, communicate reliably with the group, and step up when the team needed support?

Effort and Initiative

Was this person actively engaged during build sessions - working independently, following through on their responsibilities, and contributing beyond what was asked of them?

Professionalism

Did this person show up on time, execute their show night role effectively, and conduct themselves with professionalism throughout the event?
